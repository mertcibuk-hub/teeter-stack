# Teeter Stack — Support

**Teeter Stack** is a one-touch balancing game for iPhone. Drop shapes onto a
tilting beam, read their weight, and keep the whole thing standing.

- **Platform:** iPhone, iOS 17 or later
- **Price:** Free
- **Contact:** mertcibuk@gmail.com

We answer support email within a few business days. You can also
[open an issue](../../issues) here.

---

## How to play

1. **Aim** — drag left and right. The piece follows your thumb.
2. **Turn** — tap `TURN` to rotate the piece a quarter turn. Free and unlimited.
3. **Hold** — tap `HOLD` to shelve a piece for later. Once per piece.
4. **Drop** — lift your finger.

**Read the dots.** Every piece carries 1–4 dots. The *number* of dots is its
weight; *where* they sit is its center of mass. Heavier pieces score more but
tip the beam harder — that trade is the whole game.

**You lose when the beam touches the ground.** Nothing else ends a run. A piece
sliding off only costs you the points it earned, and breaks your streak.

**Score more by taking risk.** Placing at the center is safe but cheap. Out
near the ends the multiplier climbs to 2.5×. Consecutive clean placements build
a combo on top of that.

## Frequently asked

**Do I need an internet connection?**
No. The game is fully playable offline. The only thing that needs a connection
is the optional "Watch & Save" ad.

**Is there an account or login?**
No. Your best score, best combo, and settings live on your device only.

**What is "Watch & Save"?**
When a run ends you can optionally watch a short ad to restore your structure
and continue from where you were. Once per run. It is never required — "Play
Again" is always right there.

**How do I turn off sound or vibration?**
Settings (gear icon on the main menu) → `SOUND` / `HAPTICS`.

**How do I reset my best score?**
Settings → `RESET BEST SCORE`.

**How do I change my ad privacy choices?**
Settings → `AD PRIVACY OPTIONS`. This appears in regions where consent is
collected. See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).

**Can I change the colors?**
Yes. Settings → themes. Four presets are included and you can mix your own —
the game keeps the brightness order intact so weights stay readable.

**I found a bug.**
Email us or open an issue. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.
