# Privacy Policy — Teeter Stack

**Last updated:** 1 September 2026

This policy covers the iOS app **Teeter Stack** (bundle ID
`app.thebalancegame.thebalance`).

---

## What the game itself collects

**Nothing.**

Your best score, best combo, piece count, chosen theme, and sound/haptics
settings are stored on your device using Apple's `UserDefaults`. They are never
transmitted anywhere. There is no account, no login, no analytics SDK, and no
server we operate.

Delete the app and that data is gone with it.

---

## What the ad provider collects

Teeter Stack shows one optional rewarded video ad — the "Watch & Save" button
that appears when a run ends. It is never required to play. Ads are served by
**Google AdMob**.

When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

| Data | Why | Used to track you |
|---|---|---|
| Device identifier (advertising ID) | Serving and measuring ads | **Yes, if you allow it** |
| Approximate location, derived from IP address | Regional ad relevance | No |
| App interactions (launches, videos watched) | Ad performance | No |
| Advertising data (which ads were shown) | Ad measurement | No |
| Performance and crash data | Diagnosing SDK problems | No |

We do not receive this data ourselves in any identifiable form — we only see
aggregate earnings reports in the AdMob dashboard.

Google's own policy governs this data:
- [How Google uses information from partner sites and apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

---

## Your choices

**Tracking.** On first launch iOS asks whether Teeter Stack may track you across
other companies' apps and websites. **Declining changes nothing about the game.**
You still get the same features and the same "Watch & Save" option — the ads are
simply less targeted. You can change this any time in
iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

**Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland).** Where the law requires it, a consent form
appears before any ad is requested. If consent is not given, the ad SDK is not
started at all. You can reopen this form any time from the game's
Settings → `AD PRIVACY OPTIONS`.

**Opting out entirely.** Simply never tap "Watch & Save". No ad is requested
until you do.

---

## Children

Teeter Stack is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided
personal information through this app, contact us and we will address it.

---

## Data retention and deletion

We hold no user data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Data held
by Google is subject to Google's retention policies, linked above. To remove
everything associated with the app on your device, delete the app.
